BGTech Systems Products
Healthcare & Patient Flow

Clinic, hospital and queue management

From the reception desk to discharge: registration, consultations, labs, pharmacy, billing, wards and patient queues on one system.

Outpatient care

BG Clinic

Complete outpatient clinic management, from the front desk to the doctor’s room, lab and pharmacy counter.

  • Patient registration and records
  • Appointments and scheduling
  • Triage and vitals
  • Doctor Desk for consultations
  • Diagnosis and e-prescriptions
  • Lab orders and results
  • Pharmacy with batch-wise stock
  • Billing, receipts and refunds
Inpatient care

BG Hospital

Inpatient and ward management for hospitals, built on the same patient records as BG Clinic.

  • Admissions
  • Ward, room and bed management
  • Bed transfers
  • Doctor round notes
  • Nursing charts
  • Medication orders and administration
  • Discharge summaries
  • Nurse shifts and rostering
Queue management

BG Queue

Token-based queue management for hospitals, banks and government service centers.

  • Self-service token kiosks
  • Counter and room assignment
  • Live display screens
  • Priority queues
  • Patient flow routing between departments
  • Wait-time estimates
  • Queue analytics and reports
  • Works with BG Clinic or on its own

Security, People & Operations

Security, visitors, HR attendance and inventory

Secure your premises, manage visitors and staff attendance, and keep stock under control, all with the same logins and permissions.

Integrated security

BG Secure

Connects on-site NVRs, cameras and access controllers through a secure, outbound-only Site Agent.

  • CCTV and NVR integration
  • Video analytics: occupancy and traffic counts
  • Access control and schedules
  • Alarms and security incidents
  • Guard patrols
Visitor management

BG Visitor

Know who is on your premises, why they are there, and when they leave.

  • Visitor registration
  • Digital visitor passes
  • Access groups and schedules
  • Vehicle entry logs
  • Vehicle presence tracking at the gate
HR attendance

BG Workforce

Staff attendance synced from biometric terminals across departments and locations.

  • Biometric terminal sync
  • Shifts and timetables
  • Attendance policies
  • Holidays and leave management
  • Departments, areas and positions
Inventory & procurement

BG Asset & Inventory

Stock and purchasing across one or many warehouses.

  • Warehouses and categories
  • Suppliers and purchases
  • Goods receipts
  • Batch tracking
  • Stock transfers and adjustments

BG AI & Integration

OCR and document processing, AI chat over your organization’s knowledge base, and predictions. Connect other systems through REST APIs, API keys, webhooks, HL7, FHIR and MQTT.

BG Platform Mobile

Android and iPhone app for staff with Face ID and fingerprint unlock, role-based home screens, and a mobile Doctor Desk for calling patients and completing visits.

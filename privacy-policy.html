Privacy Policy

How BGTech Systems collects, uses, and protects information gathered through this website.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BGTech Systems (“BGTech,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with this website (bgtechsystems.com, referred to as the “Site”). It does not cover data handling within the software systems we build and deploy for individual clients, which is governed by separate agreements with those clients.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following limited ways:

  • Information you provide directly: If you contact us through the contact form, by email, by phone, or on WhatsApp, we receive whatever information you choose to share, such as your name, organization, email address, phone number, the products you are interested in, and the content of your message.
  • Automatically collected data: This Site does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own.

3. Third-Party Services

This Site loads the “Inter” typeface from Google Fonts. As a technical consequence of loading a font from Google’s servers, your browser sends a request to Google that may include your IP address and browser information. This is standard behavior for any site using Google Fonts and is governed by Google’s own privacy policy, not ours.

The contact form is delivered to us by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your submission to our email inbox. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, your conversation is handled by WhatsApp under Meta’s privacy policy. These services process the information only to deliver your message to us.

4. How We Use Information

Information you send us directly is used only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request;
  • Provide information about our services; and
  • Maintain records of business correspondence.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share information collected through this Site with third parties, except where required by law or to the extent necessary to respond to a legal request.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information sent to us, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information sent via email.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request that we delete correspondence or information you have previously sent us. We will honor such requests unless we are legally required to retain the information.

8. Children’s Privacy

This Site is intended for business and organizational audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@bgtechsystems.com or +91 9743876871.

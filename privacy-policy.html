Privacy Policy

How Nextosis Technologies processes personal data on this website and in Nextosis Platform, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nextosis Technologies (“Nextosis,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) processes personal data when you visit this website (nextosis.com, the “Site”), contact us, or use Nextosis Platform and its mobile apps (the “Platform”). It is our notice under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (the “DPDP Rules”), and our privacy policy under Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Rule 4 of the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (the “SPDI Rules”). The DPDP Act and DPDP Rules come into force in phases; until each provision applies, the SPDI Rules continue to govern the matters it covers.

1. Whose Data This Policy Covers, and Our Role

  • Visitors and people who contact us (Section 2): Nextosis is the Data Fiduciary.
  • Our customers and their users: the people who sign in to the Platform and the contacts who manage an organization’s subscription (Section 3). Nextosis is the Data Fiduciary for their account, billing and support data.
  • People whose data our customers keep in the Platform: for example a clinic’s patients, an office’s visitors, or an organization’s staff (Section 4). For this data our customer is the Data Fiduciary, and Nextosis is a Data Processor acting only on the customer’s instructions under a written agreement.

2. Visitors and Enquiries

We collect only what is needed for the purposes below:

  • Demo and enquiry requests (contact form): your name, work email, and optionally your organization, phone number, the products you are interested in, your deployment preference, and your message. Purpose: to respond to your enquiry, arrange a demonstration, send the product brochure you request, and discuss our products with you. Your request is recorded in our own customer records, with our notes of calls, meetings and follow-ups, so our team can respond.
  • Email, phone and WhatsApp: the details you choose to share, such as your name, number or email address and the content of your messages. Purpose: to respond to you. Enquiries are recorded in our customer records in the same way.
  • Technical data: when you visit the Site, our hosting and network providers automatically process your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the pages and protect the Site. Purpose: to operate and secure the Site.

The Site does not use analytics tools, advertising trackers or cookies of its own.

3. Platform Accounts, Subscriptions and Payments

  • Account data: name, work email, phone number, organization, role, a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never visible to anyone at Nextosis), two-factor authentication settings, and a sign-in history (time, IP address and device). Purpose: to give you secure access to the Platform, keep your organization’s account safe, and investigate misuse.
  • Subscription and billing data: the organization’s name, address and billing contact, the plan, licence and invoices, and for online payments the payment gateway’s order and payment references, amount and status. Purpose: to provide the plan you subscribe to, bill for it and keep the records the law requires.
  • Payments: online payments are made on the secure checkout of our payment gateway, Razorpay. Your card, UPI, net banking or wallet details are entered there and processed by Razorpay and your bank; Nextosis does not receive or store them. Razorpay’s own privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Support and service messages: the requests you send us and our replies, and messages about your account (for example sign-in codes, renewal reminders and receipts) by email, SMS or WhatsApp. We send promotional messages only with your consent, and you can opt out at any time.

The Platform uses a cookie and your browser’s storage only to keep you signed in and to remember settings such as light or dark mode. It does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not use personal data for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Data We Process for Our Customers

Organizations use the Platform to manage their own operations, and may keep in it personal data such as patients’ and visitors’ details, medical records, appointments, bills, staff and attendance records, photographs and security footage. For this data:

  • the organization decides what is collected and why, and is responsible for giving notice and obtaining any consent required (including a parent’s or lawful guardian’s consent for anyone under 18);
  • Nextosis processes it only to provide the Platform to that organization, on its instructions and under our agreement with it, and keeps it confidential;
  • we do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it for our own purposes, and we do not create or sell anonymised datasets from it;
  • the Platform gives each organization the tools to publish its privacy notice and to act on people’s requests to access, correct, export or erase their data; and
  • if you want to exercise your rights over this data, please contact the organization concerned. If you contact us, we will pass your request to it.

5. Sensitive Personal Data

Under the SPDI Rules, passwords, financial information such as bank account or payment instrument details, physical, physiological and mental health conditions, medical records and history, and biometric information are “sensitive personal data or information”. We collect sensitive personal data for our own purposes only where Section 3 describes it (passwords, and payment references), with your express consent. Health records and any biometric data in the Platform belong to the customer organization under Section 4 and are protected as described in Section 11.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, given for a specified purpose, or for the specified purpose for which you voluntarily provided it, as permitted under Sections 6 and 7(a) of the DPDP Act, and where the law requires us to (for example, to keep tax and accounting records). Using the Site alone is not treated as consent. Providing your data is voluntary, but we cannot respond to an enquiry or provide the Platform without the details needed for it.

7. Withdrawing Consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing info@nextosis.com with the subject “Withdraw consent”. We will then stop processing your personal data for that purpose and erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew, and it may mean we can no longer provide the Platform to you.

8. Service Providers (Sub-processors)

We run the Platform and keep our customer records on our own servers in India. We also use the following service providers, who process personal data on our behalf only to provide their service, under obligations of confidentiality and security:

  • Razorpay (payment gateway): processes online payments for subscriptions.
  • Brevo (email delivery): delivers the emails the Platform sends, such as sign-in codes, alerts, receipts and visitor passes.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and our SMS gateway and telecom operators: deliver WhatsApp and SMS messages, and conversations if you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Microsoft Azure Speech: turns queue-display announcements (such as a token number and counter) into speech when a screen’s browser cannot.
  • Cloudflare (network delivery and security) and GitHub (Site hosting): process IP addresses and request information.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the “Inter” typeface; your browser’s request may include your IP address and browser information.
  • Our email provider: stores the enquiries and emails we receive.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where required by law, or with a successor if our business is reorganized or sold, who will be bound by this policy.

9. Transfers Outside India

Some of these providers process data on servers outside India. Any such transfer is made only as permitted under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, and not to any country or territory restricted by the Government of India.

10. How Long We Keep Personal Data

  • Enquiries and correspondence: only as long as needed to respond and continue a discussion you started, and no more than three years after our last contact with you.
  • Platform accounts: while the account is in use; after an account is closed we erase its account data, except sign-in and security records kept to investigate misuse and records the law requires us to keep.
  • Billing and payment records: for the period required by tax and accounting laws (generally up to eight years).
  • Data we process for customers: as the customer instructs; when our agreement ends, we return or delete it as the customer instructs, unless the law requires us to keep it.

If you withdraw consent or ask us to erase your data, we will do so sooner, subject to legal requirements.

11. How We Protect Personal Data

We follow documented information security practices with managerial, technical, operational and physical controls suited to the data we handle, as required by Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules and the DPDP Rules. They include encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored only as one-way hashes, encryption of stored secrets such as access keys, two-factor authentication, role-based access so each person sees only what their work needs, audit logs of who changed what, and access to our systems limited to staff who need it and are bound to confidentiality. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the affected individuals and the Data Protection Board of India in the manner and within the time required by the DPDP Rules, report cyber security incidents to CERT-In within the time it prescribes, and, where the breach concerns a customer’s data, inform that customer without delay so that it can meet its own obligations.

12. Your Rights

For personal data we hold as Data Fiduciary, you have the right to:

  • Access: obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing we carry out, and the identities of those we have shared it with;
  • Correction and erasure: have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected, completed or updated, and have data erased when it is no longer needed for the purpose or you withdraw consent;
  • Grievance redressal: raise a grievance with us about how we handle your personal data; and
  • Nomination: nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any right, email info@nextosis.com with the subject “Data protection request”. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on the request, and we will respond within the period prescribed by law. For data an organization keeps in the Platform, please contact that organization (Section 4).

14. Your Duties

When you share personal data with us, please make sure it is accurate, do not impersonate anyone, and do not share another person’s personal data without their authority, in line with Section 15 of the DPDP Act.

15. Children

The Site and our services are intended for business and organizational customers and are not directed at children (anyone under 18). We do not knowingly collect children’s personal data for our own purposes; if you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will erase it. Where an organization keeps a child’s data in the Platform (for example, a young patient), the organization obtains the consent of the child’s parent or lawful guardian as Section 4 describes.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the law or in how we work. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we will tell our customers by email or a notice in the Platform before it takes effect, and where the law requires, we will ask for your consent again.

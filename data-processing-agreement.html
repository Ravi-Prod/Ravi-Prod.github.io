Data Processing Agreement

A template agreement between Nextosis Technologies (Data Processor) and a customer organization (Data Fiduciary), governing personal data processed in Nextosis Platform under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This is a template. It is designed to be filled in, signed by both parties and kept on file, or printed for a wet-ink signature — it is not itself a notice we publish about our own processing (that is our Privacy Policy). The blanks below (shown with a dotted underline) are completed with the customer organization’s own details before signing.

  1. Nextosis Technologies, with its registered office at #150, Burudukunte, Suguru Post, Hiriyuru Taluk, Chitradurga, Karnataka, India – 577546 (“Nextosis” or the “Processor”); and
  2.  , with its registered office at   (“Customer” or the “Fiduciary”),

each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

Customer has subscribed to Nextosis Platform under an order form, subscription agreement or similar agreement with Nextosis dated   (the “Principal Agreement”). In the course of providing Nextosis Platform, Nextosis processes personal data on Customer’s behalf and on its instructions. This DPA sets out the terms that apply to that processing, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (the “DPDP Rules”). This DPA supplements, and forms part of, the Principal Agreement. If the two conflict on a data protection matter, this DPA governs. Terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Principal Agreement.

1. Definitions

  • Consent Manager, Data Fiduciary, Data Principal, Data Processor, Personal Data, Personal Data Breach and Processing have the meanings given in the DPDP Act.
  • Board means the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Platform means Nextosis Platform and its mobile apps, as subscribed to under the Principal Agreement, including the specific Nextosis product modules (for example Clinic, Hospital, Queue, Secure, Visitor, Workforce, or Asset & Inventory) Customer has activated.
  • Customer Personal Data means personal data that Customer or its users submit to, or generate in, the Platform about Customer’s own data principals (for example its patients, visitors, staff or contractors), for which Customer is the Data Fiduciary. It does not include data for which Nextosis is itself the Data Fiduciary (such as Customer’s own account, billing and support data), which our Privacy Policy covers.
  • Sub-processor means a third party Nextosis engages to process Customer Personal Data in providing the Platform, as listed in Annex B.

2. Roles of the Parties

For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the Data Fiduciary and Nextosis is the Data Processor, processing it only to provide the Platform and only on Customer’s documented instructions, as set out in this DPA, the Principal Agreement, and Customer’s configuration and use of the Platform. Nextosis does not decide the purpose or means of processing Customer Personal Data.

3. Scope and Purpose of Processing

Nextosis processes Customer Personal Data only to: (a) make the Platform’s features available to Customer and its users; (b) provide support and maintenance; (c) keep the Platform secure and investigate misuse; and (d) comply with law. Annex A describes the categories of data principals, personal data and processing activities this covers for the Platform modules Customer has activated. Processing continues for the term of the Principal Agreement, as Section 10 (Term) describes.

4. Customer’s Instructions

Customer’s instructions are given through the Principal Agreement, this DPA, Customer’s configuration of the Platform (for example the fields, forms, visit types, or consent and acknowledgement requirements it sets up), and Customer’s written requests to Nextosis support. Nextosis will tell Customer promptly if, in its opinion, an instruction breaches the DPDP Act or another law, and may suspend that instruction until the Parties resolve the issue. Customer is responsible for: giving data principals the notice the DPDP Act requires; obtaining any consent needed (including a parent’s or lawful guardian’s consent for a data principal under 18); and having a lawful basis for every category of personal data it asks Nextosis to process.

5. Confidentiality

Nextosis limits access to Customer Personal Data to the staff and contractors who need it to provide the Platform, and binds them to confidentiality obligations at least as strict as this DPA. Nextosis will not disclose Customer Personal Data to any other person except a Sub-processor under Section 7, or as this DPA, the Principal Agreement or law requires.

6. Security Measures

Nextosis maintains reasonable security safeguards appropriate to the Customer Personal Data it processes, consistent with Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and its own documented information security practices, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data in transit;
  • passwords stored only as one-way hashes, and encryption of stored secrets such as access keys;
  • role-based access, so each user sees only the data their role needs, and two-factor authentication for Platform sign-in;
  • audit logs of who changed what within the Platform;
  • access to Nextosis’s own systems limited to staff who need it; and
  • reasonable monitoring to detect, and procedures to respond to, a Personal Data Breach.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Nextosis does not guarantee that these measures will prevent every possible breach.

7. Sub-processors

Customer authorizes Nextosis to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex B to process Customer Personal Data, under a written contract imposing data protection obligations consistent with this DPA. Nextosis remains responsible to Customer for a Sub-processor’s acts and omissions as it is for its own. If Nextosis adds or replaces a Sub-processor that will process Customer Personal Data, it will update Annex B and tell Customer by email or a notice in the Platform at least 14 days before the change takes effect, except where a shorter period is needed to address a security issue. If Customer reasonably objects on data protection grounds within that period, the Parties will discuss a resolution in good faith; if none is reached, Customer may terminate the affected part of the Platform’s service as its sole remedy.

8. Assisting with Data Principal Rights

The Platform gives Customer the tools to act directly on its data principals’ requests to access, correct, update or erase their Customer Personal Data. Where Customer cannot do this itself through the Platform, Nextosis will provide reasonable assistance, at Customer’s cost if the request needs non-standard engineering work. If a data principal contacts Nextosis directly about Customer Personal Data, Nextosis will, without responding on the merits, forward the request to Customer promptly and tell the data principal to contact Customer.

9. Personal Data Breach Notification

If Nextosis becomes aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, it will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware, describing (to the extent then known): the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data principals and records affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it and mitigate its effects. Nextosis will cooperate reasonably with Customer’s own investigation and with any notification Customer must make to the Board or to data principals under the DPDP Rules, and will not notify data principals or the Board itself on Customer’s behalf unless Customer asks it to or the law requires it directly of Nextosis.

10. Term, Return and Deletion of Data

This DPA remains in effect for as long as Nextosis processes Customer Personal Data under the Principal Agreement. On termination or expiry of the Principal Agreement, Nextosis will, at Customer’s written choice made within 30 days: (a) make Customer Personal Data available to Customer for export in a standard format the Platform supports; and/or (b) delete it, together with any copies, within 90 days of that choice or of the 30-day period ending, whichever is earlier — except data Nextosis must keep to comply with law, which it will keep only for that purpose and under this DPA’s confidentiality and security terms until erased.

11. Cross-Border Transfers

Nextosis stores and processes Customer Personal Data on its own infrastructure in India. Where a Sub-processor listed in Annex B processes it outside India, that transfer is made only as permitted under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, and not to any country or territory the Government of India restricts.

12. Audit and Information Rights

On reasonable written notice, no more than once in a 12-month period (or more often if a Personal Data Breach has occurred), Nextosis will provide Customer with the information reasonably needed to show compliance with this DPA, including a summary of its security measures, and will allow Customer or an independent auditor it appoints to audit that compliance during business hours, subject to reasonable confidentiality protections and without access to another customer’s data. Customer bears its own costs of an audit, and Nextosis’s reasonable costs of supporting more than one audit in a 12-month period.

13. Liability

Each Party is liable for its own breach of the DPDP Act and this DPA. Nextosis will indemnify Customer against loss Customer actually incurs that a court or the Board finally determines arose from Nextosis processing Customer Personal Data outside Customer’s instructions or in breach of this DPA. This Section does not expand either Party’s total liability beyond the limits of liability set out in the Principal Agreement, which continue to apply to claims under this DPA.

14. Notices

A notice to Nextosis under this DPA is sent to info@nextosis.com, marked for the attention of the Grievance Officer. A notice to Customer is sent to the contact named in the signature block below, or another address Customer gives Nextosis in writing.

15. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of India, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Chitradurga, Karnataka, unless the Principal Agreement specifies a different forum, in which case that forum applies to this DPA as well.

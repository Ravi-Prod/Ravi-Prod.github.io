Who We Are

We Build the Platform Behind Your Daily Operations

BGTech Systems is a technology startup focused on enterprise software, healthcare automation, and integrated security.

Instead of building every system from scratch, we built BG Platform: one secure foundation for identity, permissions, audit, and notifications, with business modules on top. Clients get ready-built products that we configure to their workflows and connect to the hardware they already own, from biometric terminals to CCTV recorders.

  • Hosted or on-premises. Run on our cloud by subscription, or install under license on your own servers.
  • Hardware connected. Works with NVRs, cameras, access controllers, and attendance terminals on site.
  • Open by design. REST APIs, webhooks, HL7, and FHIR for connecting to your other systems.

Solutions Built on BG Platform

One modular enterprise platform powering healthcare, queue, security, workforce, and inventory products, available as SaaS or on-premises to suit each client.

Flagship Platform

BG Platform

A modular, multi-tenant enterprise platform. Each product below is assembled from shared, reusable modules: identity, organization, role-based permissions, notifications, audit, licensing, and subscriptions.

SaaS (Cloud)

Hosted and managed by BGTech on a subscription. Fast to start, with updates, backups, and monitoring handled for you.

On-Premises

Installed on your own servers under a license, for organizations that need full control of their data and infrastructure.

BG Queue

Token-based queue management for hospitals, banks, and government service centers, with self-service kiosks, counter and display screens, priority queues, patient flow routing, and wait-time estimates.

Integrated security management that connects on-site NVRs, cameras, and access controllers through a secure, outbound-only Site Agent. Includes CCTV video analytics, occupancy and traffic counts, alarms, incidents, and guard patrols.

  • Video Analytics

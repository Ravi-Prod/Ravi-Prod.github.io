Video Analytics

Your existing cameras become an extra pair of eyes. The video never leaves your building; you just get the alerts and counts that matter.

  • People counting and occupancy charts for each camera
  • Crowd and queue alerts when waiting areas get too busy
  • Intrusion alerts for anyone in a restricted area out of hours
  • Vehicle counting by type: car, motorcycle, bus, truck, bicycle
  • Traffic counting by direction across a counting line
  • Number plate recognition with a parking log of arrival, departure and time parked
  • Works with your cameras
  • Video stays with you
Nextosis Workforce

GPS-Based Staff Attendance

No more buddy punching or disputes over who came late. Every check-in is tied to the right place and the right face, even for staff out in the field.

  • Geofenced check-in: accepted only inside the site's GPS radius, or by scanning the site's QR code
  • Face check with a short video and a head-movement liveness test; faces are registered with consent and approved by a manager
  • QR check-in with a site code that changes every 30 seconds
  • Field duty tracking: route, distance travelled, and site arrivals and departures
  • Works offline: records are kept on the phone and uploaded when signal returns
  • Leave and attendance history on the same app
  • On their own phone
  • Right place, right face

Security & Trust

Your patients’ trust, protected

Medical records are private. Nextosis keeps them safe and helps you meet India’s data protection law, without your staff having to think about it.

Locked down

Everything is sent securely and sensitive details are stored scrambled, so even a stolen copy is useless to anyone else.

Only the right people get in

Strong passwords, an optional second step on the phone, and accounts that lock after repeated wrong tries.

Each person sees their part

Reception sees reception, nurses see their ward, and only the owner sees the money. You decide.

Your records stay yours

Your hospital’s records are kept apart from everyone else’s, always.

Every change recorded

Who changed a bill, opened a record or signed a page, and when. A great help for NABH and other inspections.

Nothing is ever lost

A full backup every night, checked before it is kept.

We keep watch

We watch the system around the clock and act before a problem reaches your desk.

Safe camera connections

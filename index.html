Who We Are

We Build the Platform Behind Your Daily Operations

Video Analytics

AI models run on-site through the Nextosis Site Agent and work with your existing CCTV cameras and NVRs. Video stays on your premises; only events and counts go to the dashboard.

  • People counting and occupancy charts for each camera
  • Crowd and queue alerts when waiting areas get too busy
  • Intrusion alerts for anyone in a restricted area out of hours
  • Vehicle counting by type: car, motorcycle, bus, truck, bicycle

Nextosis Workforce

GPS-Based Staff Attendance

Staff check in from the Nextosis mobile app on Android and iPhone, alongside the biometric terminals you already use, with every check-in tied to a real place and a real face.

  • Geofenced check-in: accepted only inside the site's GPS radius, or by scanning the site's QR code
  • Face check with a short video and a head-movement liveness test; faces are registered with consent and approved by a manager
  • QR check-in with a site code that changes every 30 seconds
  • Field duty tracking: route, distance travelled, and site arrivals and departures
  • Works offline: records are kept on the phone and uploaded when signal returns
  • Leave and attendance history on the same app
  • Android & iPhone
  • Geofencing
  • Face verification

Security & Trust

Enterprise-Grade Security, Built In

Built for hospitals, government offices and businesses that handle sensitive records. Security is part of the platform core, not an add-on.

Encryption

All traffic over HTTPS with HSTS. Sensitive data such as photos, device passwords and API secrets is encrypted at rest, and passwords are never stored in readable form.

Secure Sign-In

Two-step verification with authenticator apps, account lockout after failed attempts, sign-in rate limiting and password policies set per organization.

Role-Based Access

Fine-grained permissions for every screen and action, with roles defined by each organization, so people see only what their job needs.

Data Separation

Each organization's records are kept separate in a multi-tenant design, with platform-level controls that stop data crossing between clients.

Complete Audit Trail

Every significant action is recorded with who, what and when, and audit reports can be exported for compliance reviews.

Verified Backups

Automated nightly database backups, each checked for integrity before it is kept, kept on a retention schedule.

Monitoring & Alerts

Server health, availability and performance are monitored continuously, with alerts when anything needs attention.

Secure Site Connectivity

The Site Agent connects your cameras and controllers using outbound connections only: no open firewall ports, port forwarding or public IP needed.

Your Data, Your Choice

Choose on-premises deployment to keep every record inside your own network, or our cloud for a fully managed service. The features are the same.
